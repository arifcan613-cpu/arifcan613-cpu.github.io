Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Who is responsible

Arifcan Künier, Germany. Email: arifcan613@gmail.com

2. What Faktenshorts is

Faktenshorts is a private software tool operated by Arifcan Künier. It creates short educational videos and publishes them to the operator's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts using the official APIs of these platforms. The tool is not offered to the public, and no other people sign in to it.

3. Data accessed through platform APIs

When the operator connects his own accounts, Faktenshorts receives access tokens from the platforms and uses them only to:

  • YouTube (Google): upload videos with title, description and tags, and read the channel name to confirm the connection (scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly).
  • Instagram (Meta): publish Reels with a caption and check the publishing limit of the connected Instagram professional account.
  • TikTok: read basic account information (display name, privacy options) and upload or publish videos to the connected account (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish).

Faktenshorts does not read, collect or store data about viewers, followers, comments or messages.

4. Storage and security

Access and refresh tokens are stored only locally on the operator's computer in a configuration file and a local database. They are never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No analytics or tracking is used in the app.

5. Google API Services

Faktenshorts' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, Meta in the Facebook settings under "Business integrations", and TikTok under Settings → Security → "Manage app permissions". Revoking access makes the stored tokens useless; they are also deleted from the local configuration on request. Published videos can be deleted directly on each platform.
