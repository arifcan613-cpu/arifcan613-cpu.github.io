Short videos. Real facts.

Faktenshorts creates short educational videos (30–50 seconds) about science, history, nature and technology, published on YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok.

How it works

  • Every fact in a video is checked against at least two independent, reputable sources. The sources are listed in each video's description.
  • The narration voice is generated with text-to-speech, and scripts are written with AI assistance. Videos are labeled as AI-generated wherever the platforms provide this option.
  • Footage comes from royalty-free stock libraries (Pixabay). Background music is generated for each video.

About this app

Faktenshorts is also the name of the private software that produces these videos and uploads them to the creator's own accounts through the official YouTube, Instagram and TikTok APIs. It is not offered to other users.
